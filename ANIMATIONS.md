# ANIMATIONS.md — reproduce the original evecoo landing animations (and polish them)

Source of truth: the owner's screen recordings of the ORIGINAL site, extracted as 2 fps frames in
`reference/anim/<section>/f###.jpg` (plus `recording.mov`; extract more frames with ffmpeg if you need finer timing).
View the frames in order before coding. Match layout, motion, timing and density; then make it smoother if you can.

Global rules
- English copy via `messages/en.json` (i18n test must stay green; no hardcoded strings). EUR, generic business names (e.g. "Momo's Kebab", "Brew Coffee", "Pizza House", "Burgers", "Barber Kings", "Sushi Kaito", "Bakehouse", "Nail Lounge", "Coffee House").
- Draw everything with HTML/CSS/inline SVG. No photos, no copied logos/images from the original; illustrated food/stamp icons and avatars are fine (simple SVG). No new runtime deps.
- Animations: CSS keyframes and/or a small client component with a timer (`'use client'`, `useEffect` + `setInterval`/`requestAnimationFrame`). Pause when off-screen (IntersectionObserver) and when `document.hidden`. `prefers-reduced-motion`: show a static, complete, readable state (no motion). Everything visible at rest (never opacity 0 waiting for JS). No layout shift; fixed-size stages.
- Mobile 390px must work (scale the phone/stage, no horizontal scroll).
- Easing: cubic-bezier(0.16,1,0.3,1). Phone mockup: iPhone-style frame (black bezel, dynamic island, 9:41 status bar), soft shadow.

## 1. Hero flow (`reference/anim/hero`) — component Hero.tsx visual
Wide stage under the CTA. Two curved funnel lines (top/bottom) converge to the center where a vertical glowing blue beam stands with the round brand logo node in the middle. Left label "ONE-TIME GUESTS" (grey, letter-spaced), right label "REGULARS" (brand blue).
Continuous loop: grey outline person icons (white circles) drift slowly from the left toward the beam; grey chips float on the left ("Walk-in", "First visit", "One-time guest"). When a person reaches the beam the beam pulses, the logo node flashes, and the person becomes a colored illustrated avatar with a badge (green check or blue person badge) that continues to the right and fades out near the right edge. White bold chips with the small logo appear near the beam on the right side and drift/fade: "Comes back", "+1 stamp", "New regular". Several actors at different depths/speeds at once (3-5 visible), staggered so it never looks empty. Soft lavender-to-light-blue background gradient.

## 3. How it works (`reference/anim/howitworks`) — HowItWorks.tsx (most important)
Auto-playing stepper story, ~20s loop, user can click a step to jump. Top: 3 step circles joined by lines: "Save card", "Give stamps", "Send message". Active circle = filled blue with number; the connector line to the next step fills blue as progress during the step; completed steps turn into blue circles with a check and blue labels. Below: one large centered phone that changes screens with smooth transitions:
Step 1 (Save card), scenes in order: (a) phone camera viewfinder looking at a counter standee (illustrated shop counter, standee card with business name, "Collect stamps on every visit", QR, small Apple/Google wallet badges, URL pill "evecoo.de"), camera UI with modes row (SLO-MO VIDEO PHOTO PORTRAIT PANO) and shutter; a yellow focus box around the QR, then (b) the join web page: brand-colored patterned header with round logo, "Get your stamp card", reward line "After 10 stamps you get: 1 free kebab", Name field and birthday field that TYPE IN ("Lena", "14/06/1998"), consent checkbox ticks, black "Add to Apple Wallet" button presses, then (c) the Wallet app: "Wallet" title, the brand-red pass with 10 faded stamp icons, holder name, QR, other passes peeking at the bottom (stacked colored edges).
Step 2 (Give stamps): (a) full-screen brand-blue scan confirmation with check-circle icon, "STAMP ADDED" and big "1/10"; (b) back to the Wallet pass: stamp icons fill one by one (food icon replaces faded slot, small pop animation) up to 10/10; (c) a notification banner drops in at the top "Momo's Kebab · Your reward is ready · Show the card on your next visit".
Step 3 (Send message): lock screen (blue gradient, "Thursday, November 20", big 9:41); notifications drop in one by one and stack (newest on top): "Lunch deal: until 2pm every kebab with a drink...", "Only 2 stamps left: two more visits to your free kebab", "We miss you: come by this week, your kebab is waiting".
Then loop back to step 1 (reset stepper). Phone stays same size; screen content cross-fades/slides.
